# Cellulitis (Skin Infection)

## What is wrong?
- You have a skin infection caused by germs called bacteria.
- It often starts after a cut, scrape, bite, or rash.
- It can make skin red, swollen, warm, and painful.
- You may also have fever, chills, or an upset stomach.

## What should I do at home?
- Take all your antibiotics, even after you feel better.
- Raise an infected arm or leg on pillows to ease pain and swelling.
- Rest the area. Stay out of pools and hot tubs.
- If it drains, keep it covered with the dressing you were told to use.
- Do not put hydrogen peroxide or alcohol on it. They slow healing.

## When should I feel better?
- It may take 2 to 3 days to look better.
- It may take up to a week to go away.
- To prevent it, clean cuts with soap and water and cover them.
- If you have diabetes, check your feet and legs every day for cuts.

## See a doctor if:
- You are not getting better after 2 or 3 days.

## Go to a walk-in clinic or the Emergency Department right away if:
- Pain, swelling, warmth, or redness gets worse.
- You see red streaks spreading from the area, or pus.
- You have a fever over 38°C, chills, or sweats.
- You feel sick all over, with an upset stomach or weakness.

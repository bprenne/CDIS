# Upper Respiratory Tract Infection (Common Cold)

## What is wrong?
- A virus infected your nose, sinuses, throat, or windpipe.
- You may have a runny or stuffy nose, sneezing, or cough.
- You may have a sore throat or a low fever.
- Colds spread by coughs, sneezes, and touch.
- Antibiotics do not help colds, because a virus causes them.

## What should I do at home?
- Get lots of sleep, eat healthy food, and drink lots of fluids.
- Take acetaminophen (Tylenol) or ibuprofen for fever or aches.
- Use salt water (saline) nose drops or spray for a stuffy nose.
- Use a humidifier in your bedroom to add moisture to the air.
- Do not smoke or vape.

## When should I feel better?
- Most colds get better in about a week.
- Some symptoms, like cough, can last 2 or 3 weeks.

## See a doctor if:
- You do not feel better in a week.

## Go to a walk-in clinic or the Emergency Department right away if:
- You have trouble breathing.
- You have chest pain.
- You have a new or higher fever.
- You have a bad headache, or neck pain or stiffness.
- You are dizzy, weak, confused, or tired all the time.

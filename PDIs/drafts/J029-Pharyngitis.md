# Pharyngitis (Sore Throat)

## What is wrong?
- Most sore throats come from a virus or bacteria.
- Smoke, dry air, pollution, allergies, or yelling can also cause one.
- You may have fever, swollen neck glands, runny nose, cough, or hoarse voice.
- Antibiotics only help if bacteria caused it.

## What should I do at home?
- Drink lots of cool fluids, and rest.
- Suck on throat lozenges.
- Gargle with mouthwash or warm salt water a few times a day.
- For salt water, mix 1/2 to 1 teaspoon of salt in 1 cup of warm water.
- Take acetaminophen (Tylenol) or ibuprofen for pain.

## When should I feel better?
- Most sore throats get better in about a week.

## See a doctor if:
- You do not feel better in a week.

## Go to the Emergency Department right away if:
- You have trouble breathing.
- Your sore throat gets worse on one side.
- You have new or worse trouble swallowing.
- You have a new or higher fever.

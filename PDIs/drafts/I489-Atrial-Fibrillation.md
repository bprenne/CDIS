# Atrial Fibrillation (A-Fib)

## What is wrong?
- Your heart beats unevenly, and often too fast.
- It can cause blood clots that travel to the brain and cause a stroke.
- It can lead to chest pain or heart failure.
- A fast heartbeat can make you weak, dizzy, or short of breath.
- In the Emergency Department, you may have had a shock or IV medicine to fix your heart rhythm.

## What should I do at home?
- Take your medicines exactly as prescribed, every day.
- Your medicine may slow your heart or keep its rhythm steady.
- If you got a blood thinner, take it exactly as prescribed. It helps prevent stroke.
- Do not smoke. Exercise, eat healthy food, and sleep 7 to 9 hours.
- If you drink alcohol, have no more than 2 drinks a week.

## When should I feel better?
- You should feel better in a few days.
- Keep taking your medicine. Do not stop it on your own.

## See a doctor if:
- You think you are having a problem with your medicine.
- See your family doctor soon for a check-up.
- If you are not sure whether to go in, call HealthLine at 811.

## Go to the Emergency Department right away if:
- You have chest pain.
- You are short of breath.
- Your heart is beating fast or unevenly.
- You feel your heart needs to be checked.

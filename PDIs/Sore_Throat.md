# Sore Throat (Pharyngitis)

## What is wrong?

- Infection by bacteria or a virus causes most sore throats. Cigarette smoke, dry air, air pollution, allergies, and yelling can also cause a sore throat. Sore throats can be painful and annoying. Fortunately, most sore throats go away on their own. If you have a bacterial infection, your doctor may prescribe antibiotics.

## What to expect?

- Depending on the cause of your sore throat, you may have a fever, swelling in your neck glands, a runny nose, a cough, itchy eyes, or a hoarse voice
- Most of the time, a sore throat isn’t a serious medical issue. Most sore throats go away within a week or so. You may need antibiotics if your sore throat is caused by a bacterial infection.
- To help manage the pain you can drink lots of cool fluids, rest, and suck on throat lozenges
- You can try gargling with a mouthwash or warm salt water (1/2 – 1 teaspoon of salt in 1 cup of warm water) several times a day
- You can also take acetaminophen (Tylenol) or ibuprofen for throat pain

## When should I feel better?

- Most sore throats feel better in a week or so.
- If you aren’t feeling better in a week, you should be seen again

## Go to a walk-in clinic or the Emergency Department right away if:

- You have trouble breathing
- Your sore throat gets worse on one side
- You have a new or worse trouble swallowing
- You have a new or higher fever
